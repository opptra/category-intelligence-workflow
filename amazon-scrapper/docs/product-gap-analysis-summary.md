# Product Gap Analysis — Quick Summary

A scannable digest of the full framework (`product-gap-analysis-framework.md`). **60 attributes, 10 pillars.** Each row = **which** attribute, **why** it matters, **how** to evaluate it, and its **weight (1–10)**.

**Weight key:** 9–10 critical · 7–8 high · 5–6 moderate · 3–4 supporting.
**Gates** (must pass, or everything else is void): Category Placement, Compliance, Stock Availability.
